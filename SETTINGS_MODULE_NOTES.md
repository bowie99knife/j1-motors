# J1 Workshop OS — Polished Settings Module

Route: `/admin/settings`

Included:
- General business identity and regional settings
- Workshop address, labour rate, GST and default terms
- Branding, logo preview, colours, PDF header/footer and email signature
- Quote numbering, expiry, notes and terms
- Invoice numbering, payment terms, bank details and footer
- Booking intervals, duration, buffer and daily capacity
- Copy-ready customer message templates
- User role preparation for future authentication
- Session preferences and settings export
- Local browser persistence, dirty-state tracking, save/discard actions and toast feedback

Packaging checks:
- No `public/src`
- No `.next`
- No `node_modules`
- No duplicated source tree
